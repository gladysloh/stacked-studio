# Colour and mood

## Palette
Name 3–6 colours precisely, including the background or surface colour, the shadow colour and any accent: "muted sage", "warm dark amber with a red undertone", "bone white", "deep teal shadows". Precise names beat "green", "brown" and "dark".

**Brand colours:** use hex for the brand's own colours and say their role: *ground* (background or surface), *accent* (a prop, a light gel, a ribbon) or *product* (already carried by the photo, so don't restate). Keep the scene from fighting the product's own colours.

## Temperature, saturation and contrast
- **Temperature:** warm (amber and gold in the neutrals) · cool (blue or grey neutrals) · neutral.
- **Saturation:** desaturated · muted · natural · pushed.
- **Contrast:** high (deep blacks, bright whites) · low (lifted blacks, matte, soft midtones).

## Grade styles
| Grade | Looks like | Common in |
|---|---|---|
| Clean digital | natural, even, no cast | skincare, tech |
| Film emulation | slightly muted, fine grain, soft halation in highlights | lifestyle, fashion |
| Matte / fade | lifted blacks, flat and painterly | beauty, wellness |
| Crushed / moody | deep blacks, high contrast, cool shadows | fragrance, spirits |
| Warm editorial | amber-gold highlights, neutral shadows | food, wellness |
| Desaturated editorial | near-monochrome | fashion, fragrance |
| Split-tone | e.g. cool shadows with warm highlights | campaign drama |

**Grain and finish:** none (clean) · subtle · visible · heavy.

## Mood
Choose **exactly five precise adjectives**. Say "austere, luminous, tactile, unhurried, intimate", not "nice, clean, beautiful". Mood words go last in the prompt; they tint the image rather than structure it.

Energy families to pick from:
- calm, meditative, slow
- bright, energetic, immediate
- moody, intimate, withdrawn
- luxurious, aspirational, restrained
- playful, expressive, spontaneous
