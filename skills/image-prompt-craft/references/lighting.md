# Lighting

Describe light as **direction + quality + source character + effect**. "Beautiful lighting" gives the model nothing; "soft window light from camera right, warm, feathered shadows" gives it a setup.

## Direction
| Direction | Reads as |
|---|---|
| front | flat, graphic, few shadows |
| side (camera left/right) | texture and dimension; strong shadow on the far side |
| back / rim | glowing edge, separation, moody |
| overhead | editorial, short shadows |
| raking low side | long shadows, golden-hour drama |
| under | rare; unsettling |

Name the **key** light, then any **fill** or **rim** separately.

## Quality
- **Hard:** a small or distant source (bare flash, direct sun). Sharp-edged, defined shadows.
- **Soft:** a large or close source (softbox, overcast sky, window). Gradual falloff, feathered shadows.

## Source character
natural window (soft, directional, neutral to warm) · overcast daylight (flat, even, cool) · studio strobe (clean, precise) · continuous LED (slightly clinical) · practical lamp or candle (warm, orange, intimate) · golden hour (warm, raking, long shadows)

## Shadows and highlights
- **Shadows:** present or absent; deep or barely there; hard or feathered; on the surface (a contact shadow grounds the product) or on the product; warm or cool.
- **Highlights:** crisp specular points vs large soft highlights; retained vs blown to white; light that wraps around curves vs light that hits flat planes.

## Named setups (good starting points)
| Setup | Prompt wording |
|---|---|
| Soft studio hero | "large softbox from upper left wrapping the product, gentle falloff, soft retained highlights" |
| Morning window | "soft directional window light from camera right, warm golden bias, feathered low-contrast shadows" |
| Dark luxury | "hard single-source side light from camera left, deep shadow plane, crisp warm specular highlights" |
| Backlit glow | "strong rim light from behind, glowing edges, dark front, halo on the surface" |
| Hard noon | "hard overhead sun, short crisp shadows, high contrast" |
| Low-key | "single low-key light, most of the frame in shadow, product edge-lit" |

## Time of day (even in studio)
early morning (soft, slightly golden) · midday (flat, bright, clinical) · golden hour (warm, raking) · dusk/evening (dim, tungsten, intimate) · timeless

## Shiny and recoloured surfaces
When the direction changes a colour on metal, glass or gloss, name the reflections too: "…and re-tint all specular highlights, flares and reflections to match". Otherwise the old colour survives in the highlights.

## Product beats in a sequence
Keep the product's finish constant across scenes: "keep the product a constant neutral finish; do not let the scene's light tint it."
