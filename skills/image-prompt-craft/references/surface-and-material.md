# Surface and material

The surface is often the **single biggest style choice** in product photography. The same bottle on travertine, wet slate or linen is three different campaigns.

## Background type
seamless / infinity curve (no horizon; studio) · environmental (a real room or outdoors, out of focus) · textured flat (paper, fabric, plaster, concrete) · gradient (adds dimension to a seamless) · solid colour (name it, with hex if it's a brand colour)

## Surface vocabulary
| Family | Terms |
|---|---|
| Stone | white Calacatta marble, grey-veined marble, travertine, terrazzo, polished black granite, wet slate, raw poured concrete |
| Wood | pale white oak, dark walnut, weathered driftwood |
| Fabric | linen (creased or smooth), velvet, raw silk, terry towel |
| Hard | white acrylic, frosted glass, clear glass shelf, ceramic tile, brushed steel |
| Natural | sand, wet pebbles, moss, water surface |

Add **finish and reflection**: matte (absorbs) vs polished or wet (reflects the product's base). A gentle mirror reflection grounds a product; a strong one competes with it.

## Material words for the hero and props
Name true materials, not vibes: "brushed gold metal", "wet glass with condensation", "matte ceramic", "frosted acrylic", "long-exposure golden light trails". The hero element's material is the fact most often misread, so take it from the reference description when there is one.

Never describe the *product's* packaging material or label: the product photo carries that (see SKILL.md §1).

## Props
- **Natural:** botanicals, stones, water droplets, ingredients.
- **Lifestyle:** towel, cup, book, tools.
- **Abstract:** geometric forms, cut paper, fabric folds.
- **Rules:**
  - Each prop earns its place by saying something about the brief (an ingredient, a ritual, a setting).
  - Keep props smaller and softer than the product.
  - Brand don'ts apply to props too: no competitor-looking packaging, no props the brand bans.

## Texture and finish
Texture celebrated (rough stone, linen grain) vs smooth and clinical. Overall finish: polished and refined · raw and organic · clinical and minimal · warm and tactile.
